# Plain-English Guide to the Multi-Agent Consultancy Framework

## 1. Meet Your AI Team

Think of this workspace as your personal, on-demand AI consulting firm:

| Agent | Plain-English Role | What They Do For You |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **@Orchestrator** | **The Project Lead (Managing Director)** | Keeps the whole project organized. Assigns tasks to the right team members, watches the budget, and summarizes the final results for you. |
| **@Consultant** | **The Business Strategist** | Analyzes your business goals, figures out how much money and time you will save, and writes clear project goals before anyone writes code. |
| **@Architect** | **The Blueprint Designer** | Designs the structure of your AI system. Decides how the AI remembers information and makes sure safety backup plans are in place. |
| **@Engineer** | **The Software Builder** | Takes the blueprint and writes clean, reliable computer code, along with automated tests to prove it works. |
| **@Auditor** | **The Quality & Safety Inspector** | Tests the system to make sure it is accurate, safe from hackers, respects user privacy, and doesn't make up fake information. |

---

## 2. Key Terms Simplified

* **EVD (Enterprise Value Delivery)**: A proven 5-step roadmap (Idea $\rightarrow$ Plan $\rightarrow$ Code $\rightarrow$ Test $\rightarrow$ Final Delivery) that guarantees you get real business value.
* **PRD (Product Requirements Document)**: A clear checklist explaining what the AI must do, how fast it needs to be, and what rules it must follow.
* **ROI (Return on Investment) & TCO (Total Cost of Ownership)**: Calculations showing how much the AI costs to run versus how many human hours and dollars it will save your business.
* **Trustworthy AI (7-Pillar Safety Check)**: A complete inspection making sure the AI is:
  1. *Fair* (no unfair bias)
  2. *Reliable* (doesn't guess or make things up)
  3. *Clear* (can explain how it got its answer)
  4. *Private* (keeps customer data confidential)
  5. *Secure* (blocked against hacker tricks)
  6. *Accountable* (keeps an audit log of everything it did)
  7. *Legal & Compliant* (follows data protection laws like HIPAA/GDPR)
